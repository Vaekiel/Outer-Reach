## Consigne de génération

Create ONE standalone production game sprite: STRATE, a LARGE size L industrial mining barge for Outer Reach Online. Image 1 atlas is the original design lineage: ONLY its top-left SILLON ship is relevant. Image 2 is the approved medium VEINE: match its exact finely textured pixel art, worn gunmetal steel panels, copper-bronze pipes and braces, restrained dark teal coolant tanks, tiny cyan glass windows, top-left lighting and crisp details. Create a clearly DISTINCT LARGE vessel, NOT a copy or enlargement of Veine and NOT a four-armed variation of it. Strict ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP-DOWN view, nose pointing RIGHT, engines LEFT. LARGE HEAVY MINING BARGE SILHOUETTE: broad long rectangular twin-pontoon industrial hull with blunt reinforced bow; two parallel banks of three massive segmented ore hoppers along upper and lower hull sides, six cargo bays total, around an exposed central longitudinal conveyor channel and strong transverse structural bridges. The empty channel and recessed machinery give an instantly readable split-hull silhouette. One compact offset command bridge on the upper forward shoulder with cyan windows, NOT a pointed central fighter cockpit. A single massive central articulated extraction gantry at the bow carrying two compact industrial cutting-laser lenses and a scoop-like collection mouth, short and integrated into the blunt bow; no two long forward shoulder arms like Veine. Along the flanks, folded ore handling cranes, visibly functional grabber clamps and loading rails. Pair of large teal cylindrical coolant reservoirs at the aft quarter, extensive finned heat exchanger banks and copper plumbing; four heavy squat stern thruster bells arranged as two pairs, plus small maneuvering jets. Scale cues: small access hatches, narrow maintenance walkways, many rivets, railings, tiny inspection windows; intended hull about 1.5 times Veine length with much greater ore storage mass. Heavy slow working ship built for hauling extracted ore, not combat, no refinery tower, no magical tractor beams. Preserve family resemblance through mechanical materials and machinery, while the barge silhouette and cargo architecture are unique. Rich fine crisp pixel clusters and stepped shadows; no smooth painting, no glossy 3D, no isometric tilt. ONE complete ship centered on a square 1536x1536 solid pure black RGB0 background with at least 8 percent clearance around all extremities. Ship approximately 84 percent canvas width and 58 percent height. Background and interior open gaps pure black; all solid ship material shadows above RGB12. No text, labels, branding, stats, frame, starfield, other objects, flames, trails or active laser beams.
